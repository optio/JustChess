### Summary

The design is implementable. Coverage of the FRs is complete, the lock handling around the search is correct, the engine queue and restart logic is coherent and testable, and seat-A-only actions and the no-user-text-to-UCI rule hold. The two Major findings are the missing game identity in the reply race (a rematch after a resign can collide with a stale reply) and the missing per-address bound on engine-consuming routes; both should be settled before Code Generation, but they stay within the two-Major threshold for READY.
