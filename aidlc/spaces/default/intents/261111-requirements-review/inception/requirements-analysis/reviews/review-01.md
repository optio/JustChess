### Summary

This is a strong, traceable spec: all 82 ids in traceability.json match requirements.md, nearly every row has a pass/fail criterion, and every answer in the questions file is reflected. It is NOT-READY because four gaps would send engineering back with questions. They are migration of existing rooms and tokens, where the seat identity lives (given push to closed pages), which side a learner plays in practice mode, and the P1 HTTPS and push-key prerequisites that sit in P2. The minor findings are mostly missing numbers and corner cases.
