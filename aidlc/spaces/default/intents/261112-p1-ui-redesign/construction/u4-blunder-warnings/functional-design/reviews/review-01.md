### Summary

The rule set and route design are sound and consistent with U1's ordering and middleware. Three gaps block implementation without guessing. The hook contract cannot carry the 400, 403 and 409 behaviour BR3.4 promises. The 300 ms budget has no mechanism and could stall the event loop. The exchange and before-position logic is undefined under check and pins.
