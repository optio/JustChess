### Summary

The U2 spec covers its requirements and the mockup values, and the geometry rules are correct. The board and settings contracts are mostly implementable. There are three Major gaps. The 3:1 piece contrast claim fails on Rosewood. The notifications hook contract is not defined. The lock transitions and animation completion are not defined. The test scope also leaves the interaction logic untestable without a DOM.
