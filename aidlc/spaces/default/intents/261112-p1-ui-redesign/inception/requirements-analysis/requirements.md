## 1. Intent analysis

P1 makes JustChess good to play and to learn with. That means:
- a shareable game that is easy to read on a phone;
- a clear result and a rematch;
- warnings before blunders;
- alerts that actually arrive;
- practice against scripted replies.

It also replaces today's generic look, which the scan describes as one dark-green palette, beige on every control, a system font, stacked rounded cards, Unicode pieces and no motion. The new look is deliberate: a classic, warm chess identity with satisfying motion (Q1).

The scan shows four of the spec's "Partial" items are actually wrong: square colours, auto-ending draws, the join alert, and HTMX still loaded. They are listed as Fix below.

## 4. Constraints

- Python 3.11+, FastAPI, Jinja2, python-chess, SQLite and uv. The frontend is server-rendered HTML with plain JavaScript and CSS, and no HTMX: its script tag is removed (spec, scan).
- The app may be split from today's single `app.py` into modules. Domain design decides how (scan).
- No accounts, payments, matchmaking or computer opponent. Stockfish is installed but unused in P1 (spec).
- Brand: **JustChess**, tagline "No accounts. No subscriptions. Just chess." (restored from the old spec's brand line; review finding).
- Third-party assets (the SVG piece set, fonts) must have licences that allow self-hosting, and they are served from the app, not from a CDN (Q3, FR10.5).

## 5. Assumptions

- The two current lessons are taught from White, the side that chooses the line. Rationale: in the French Exchange Monte Carlo line, 4.c4 is White's choice (Q7).
- Fonts are open-licence (for example from Google Fonts, under the OFL) and self-hosted. Rationale: the constraint above, and no tracking.
- "Without loss" in FR3.8 means the piece can be taken with nothing recaptured, or by a lower-value piece. Rationale: this can be detected without an engine (spec).
- The rematch offer, the abandon time and retention are measured from the server clock. Rationale: one trusted clock (corner checklist, Time).

## 6. Out of scope (this piece of work)

- Every P2 and P3 item in the spec, including resign, draw offer, branching trees, catalog expansion, Docker, rate limits and backups (spec).
- Choosing and setting up public HTTPS hosting (Q4: decide later).
- Migrating existing rooms (Q5).
- Using Stockfish in any feature (spec FR9).

## 7. Definition of done

On a phone and a desktop:
- two players start a game from a shared link;
- they play on a correctly coloured and oriented, labelled board in the new warm design, with light and dark themes and board themes;
- pieces animate, and they get blunder warnings;
- each player gets exactly one alert per opponent move, with push verified on `localhost`;
- the end-of-game overlay shows the result and reason, then a rematch with a running score.

A learner practises both lessons from data files, with the trainer replying and a side switch.

NFR9 tests pass. Tokens are hashed, and the VAPID keys are kept out of the repository.
